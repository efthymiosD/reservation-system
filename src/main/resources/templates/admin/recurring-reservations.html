Times are venue-local, aligned to the venue's half-hour booking grid within opening hours. Assigning a slot materializes each upcoming occurrence inside the selected booking window (1, 3 or 6 months) as a real reservation the customer can cancel; weeks where the slot is already taken are skipped automatically.
